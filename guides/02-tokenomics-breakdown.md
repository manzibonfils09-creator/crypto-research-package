# 📊 Guide 02 — Tokenomics: How to Read Supply & Economics

Tokenomics = Token + Economics. It's the single most important factor in whether a coin goes up or gets dumped on you.

---

## Key Metrics to Analyze

### 1. 💊 Total Supply vs Circulating Supply

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Max Supply** | The absolute maximum coins that will ever exist (e.g. Bitcoin = 21M) |
| **Total Supply** | Coins created so far (including locked ones) |
| **Circulating Supply** | Coins actually in the market right now |

**Why it matters:** A coin with 1 billion max supply and only 10M circulating means 990M coins are coming. That's massive future selling pressure.

> 🔑 Rule: Low circulating supply + high future unlock = price danger

---

### 2. 📈 Market Cap vs Fully Diluted Valuation (FDV)

- **Market Cap** = Price × Circulating Supply
- **FDV** = Price × Max Supply

If FDV is 10x the market cap, most tokens haven't hit the market yet. When they do — price pressure.

> Example: Coin at $1, 100M circulating, 2B max supply.
> Market cap = $100M. FDV = $2B. That gap is risk.

---

### 3. 🔒 Vesting Schedules & Unlocks

Who owns the tokens and when do they get them?

- **Team tokens** — usually locked 1-2 years, then unlock over time
- **Investor tokens** — VCs who bought cheap. When they unlock, they often sell
- **Community/ecosystem** — used for rewards, grants, growth

**What to check:** When are the next big unlock events? This is often when coins dump.

Where to find unlock schedules:
- [Token Unlocks](https://token.unlocks.app)
- [Vesting.app](https://vesting.app)
- The project's official docs

---

### 4. 🔥 Inflation vs Deflation

- **Inflationary** — new coins are minted over time (e.g. most PoS chains). Dilutes holders.
- **Deflationary** — coins are burned or supply is capped. Good for long-term holders.
- **Hybrid** — burns + new emissions. Need to check if burns outpace minting.

> Ethereum became deflationary after EIP-1559. ETH burned > ETH minted = supply shrinking.

---

### 5. 📦 Token Distribution

Healthy distribution looks like:
- Team: 15-20%
- Investors: 15-25%
- Community/Ecosystem: 40-50%+
- Public sale: 10-20%

Red flags:
- Team holds 40%+ — too much centralized control
- No community allocation — it's not for the people, it's for the insiders
- No public sale — early investors get rich, retail gets scraps

---

## ✅ Tokenomics Checklist

- [ ] Max supply is defined and reasonable
- [ ] Circulating supply vs max supply gap is understood
- [ ] FDV is not wildly higher than market cap
- [ ] Vesting schedules are clear — no surprise unlocks soon
- [ ] Team allocation is under 20%
- [ ] Community/ecosystem gets the biggest share
- [ ] Inflation rate is known and manageable
- [ ] Token has a burn mechanism or supply cap

# ⛓️ Guide 04 — On-Chain Analysis: Reading the Blockchain

On-chain data is the most honest signal in crypto. The blockchain doesn't lie — it shows exactly what's happening with money, wallets, and activity.

---

## What Is On-Chain Analysis?

It's the practice of reading raw blockchain data to understand:
- Where money is flowing
- Who's buying and selling
- Whether the project is actually being used
- If whales are accumulating or dumping

---

## Key On-Chain Metrics

### 1. 🐋 Whale Wallets
Large holders ("whales") control price. Track their moves:
- Are whales accumulating (buying more)?
- Are whales distributing (sending to exchanges = preparing to sell)?

**Tools:** [Nansen](https://nansen.ai) | [Arkham Intelligence](https://arkhamintelligence.com) | [Etherscan](https://etherscan.io)

---

### 2. 📊 Active Addresses
- How many unique wallets are using this blockchain daily?
- Growing active addresses = growing adoption
- Declining active addresses = people leaving

**Tools:** [Glassnode](https://glassnode.com) | [CryptoQuant](https://cryptoquant.com)

---

### 3. 💸 Exchange Flows
- **Coins moving TO exchanges** = people preparing to sell (bearish)
- **Coins moving OFF exchanges** = people holding in private wallets (bullish)

> When Bitcoin exchange reserves drop, it historically signals price appreciation.

**Tools:** [CryptoQuant](https://cryptoquant.com) | [Glassnode](https://glassnode.com)

---

### 4. 🔄 Transaction Volume
- High and growing volume = real usage
- Artificial volume can be faked — look for consistency, not spikes
- Compare volume to similar projects at the same stage

---

### 5. 📈 Total Value Locked (TVL) — for DeFi
- TVL = how much money is locked in a DeFi protocol
- Growing TVL = trust and usage growing
- Sudden TVL drop = people pulling money out (risk signal)

**Tools:** [DeFiLlama](https://defillama.com) — the best free TVL tracker

---

### 6. 👨‍💻 Developer Activity
- Is the codebase being actively improved?
- GitHub commits, pull requests, issues resolved
- A dead GitHub = a dying project

**Tools:** [Santiment](https://santiment.net) | Direct GitHub repo

---

### 7. 🏦 Token Concentration
- What % of supply is held by the top 10 wallets?
- High concentration = whales can dump and crash the price anytime
- Healthy: top 10 wallets hold under 30% (excluding team/treasury wallets)

**How to check:**
- Ethereum tokens: [Etherscan Token Holders](https://etherscan.io)
- Solana tokens: [Solscan](https://solscan.io)
- BSC tokens: [BscScan](https://bscscan.com)

## ✅ On-Chain Checklist

- [ ] Active addresses are growing or stable
- [ ] Exchange reserves are decreasing (coins leaving exchanges)
- [ ] Whale wallets are accumulating, not distributing
- [ ] Token concentration: top 10 wallets hold under 30%
- [ ] TVL is growing (for DeFi projects)
- [ ] Developer activity on GitHub is consistent
- [ ] Transaction volume is organic, not spiking artificially

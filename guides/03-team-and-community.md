# 👥 Guide 03 — Team & Community Analysis

The best technology fails with the wrong team. The worst technology can succeed with the right community. Both matter.

---

## Part A: Researching the Team

### Step 1: Are They Doxxed?
- **Doxxed** = real identities publicly known
- Anonymous teams are higher risk — no accountability if they disappear
- Exception: some privacy-focused projects (Monero) have anonymous devs but long track records

### Step 2: Verify Their LinkedIn
Search every named team member:
- Does their work history match their claims?
- Have they built anything before?
- Are their connections real (not 50 followers with no activity)?

### Step 3: Check Their GitHub
- Is the lead developer actually coding?
- How many commits in the last 30 days?
- Are issues being resolved or ignored?

> [GitHub Stats for any repo](https://github.com/user/repo/pulse)

### Step 4: Past Projects
- Have they built successful projects before?
- Or have they been involved in projects that failed or rugged?
- Search: `[founder name] crypto rug pull` or `[founder name] scam`

### Step 5: Advisors
- Are the advisors real and relevant?
- Or are they just name-dropping celebrities with no actual involvement?
- Red flag: advisors with no crypto/tech background

## Part B: Reading the Community

### Where to Look

| Platform | What to Analyze |
|---|---|
| **Twitter/X** | Engagement quality — real conversations or just bots? |
| **Discord** | Activity level, how team responds to questions |
| **Telegram** | Size vs engagement ratio |
| **Reddit** | Honest community discussions, criticism allowed? |
| **YouTube** | Who's covering it — paid shillers or genuine analysts? |

### Signs of a Healthy Community
- ✅ Critical questions are welcomed and answered
- ✅ Community members can explain the project in their own words
- ✅ Organic growth — not sudden spikes around influencer pumps
- ✅ Team is active and transparent in Discord/Telegram
- ✅ Bear market discussions still happen (not just bull hype)

### Red Flags in Community
- 🚩 Anyone asking critical questions gets banned
- 🚩 Only positive comments — dissent deleted
- 🚩 Bots flooding Telegram with "LFG 🚀🚀🚀"
- 🚩 Community only grew during a price pump
- 🚩 Team goes silent when things go wrong

### Tools to Check for Fake Followers
- [SparkToro](https://sparktoro.com) — Twitter audience quality
- [Botometer](https://botometer.osome.iu.edu) — Bot detection on Twitter
- [LunarCrush](https://lunarcrush.com) — Social sentiment and engagement metrics

---

## ✅ Team & Community Checklist

- [ ] Team is doxxed or has a long verifiable track record
- [ ] LinkedIn profiles are real and match their claimed experience
- [ ] GitHub shows active development
- [ ] No history of failed or fraudulent projects
- [ ] Community is active outside of price pumps
- [ ] Critical questions are welcomed, not censored
- [ ] Discord/Telegram has real conversations, not just hype
- [ ] Social media following is organic (check for bots)

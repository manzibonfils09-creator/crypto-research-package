## 💡 Pro Tip: Free Research Stack

If you want to go deep without spending money, use this combo:
1. **CoinGecko** — price & basic data
2. **DeFiLlama** — TVL & protocol health
3. **Etherscan/Solscan** — raw on-chain data
4. **Arkham Intelligence** — wallet tracking
5. **Rugcheck/Token Sniffer** — scam check
6. **LunarCrush** — social sentiment

That's a complete research setup for $0.
